For Tutors

Tutor Code of Conduct

Every active tutor must accept this before teaching on PeerScholars.

Treat students respectfully. Arrive on time. Only tutor approved subjects/grades. Never request unnecessary personal information. Never ask a student to keep communication secret from their parent. Do not use private social media with younger students. Do not exchange personal phone numbers unless authorized. All sessions are online via Google Meet. Report safety concerns immediately. Maintain professional boundaries. Do not share student information.
