Our Cause

Built for families. Built for the community.

We believe every child deserves great tutoring — and every high schooler deserves a chance to lead.

The Problem

Many elementary and middle school students struggle with homework and difficult subjects. Professional tutoring often costs $40–$100 per hour, making it too expensive for many families.

At the same time, high school students want community service opportunities and leadership experience — but have limited ways to earn them meaningfully.

Our Solution

PeerScholars connects younger students with trained high school tutors based on grade level, subject, location, interests, learning style, and availability.

Families pay just $8–16/hour based on tax bracket. Tutors volunteer their time and earn verified community service hours.

Parents stay involved

PeerScholars keeps families in the loop with session reports, attendance tracking, and clear pricing — so you always know how your child is doing.

Who we serve

K–8 Students & Parents

Younger students (below high school) who want to learn and grow. Parents and guardians are the customers — but the kids are who we serve.

High School Tutors

Verified high school students who don't pay — they volunteer to help younger students grow while earning community service hours and leadership experience.

The Community

Our target market: families with children in grades K–8 who need affordable tutoring, and high schoolers seeking volunteer and leadership opportunities.
