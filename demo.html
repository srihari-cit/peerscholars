Step-by-step walkthrough

Follow this walkthrough to see exactly how parents, students, and tutors use PeerScholars.

1

Create your account

Click Sign Up and choose Parent or High School Tutor. Enter your phone number — we'll call when we're free.

2

Build your profile

Add grade, subjects, city/state, interests, and availability. Tutors pick subjects & grade levels they'll teach.

3

Get matched or browse

We suggest matches by subject + interests. Or browse tutors, chat, and both agree before booking.

4

Meet & learn

Sessions online or in-person locally. After each session, parents get a progress report.

Using the app after sign-up

Chat before you match

Parents and tutors can message each other. Both must agree before a session is booked — so everyone feels comfortable.

Online or in-person

If you're in the same city, meet face-to-face for one-on-one help. Otherwise, connect over video call.

Session reports

After every session, tutors submit a short report. Parents see it instantly in their dashboard.

Track volunteer hours

Tutors log hours in their profile. Our team verifies and approves them for community college credit.
